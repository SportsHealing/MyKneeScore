Privacy

Your answers stay on your device.

We do not collect, store or share your answers or your score. This page explains what that means and the little that does happen when you visit.

In short

What we hold about you

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign up and no database.

  • Your answers are scored inside your browser. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Nothing is saved. Close or reload the page and your answers are gone. Note your score if you want to track it.
  • No cookies. No analytics. No advertising or tracking.
  • No fonts, scripts or images are loaded from other companies. Everything comes from this site.

Health information

Why this matters

The questions ask about your knee. Under UK data protection law that is health information, which deserves extra care. Our approach is simple: it never leaves your device, so we never hold it.

Hosting

What happens when you visit

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details, such as the page asked for and your browser type, so it can deliver the page and protect the service from abuse.

  • GitHub does not receive your answers or your score. Those never leave your browser.
  • We do not have access to these server logs, and we do not use them.
  • GitHub handles this data under its own privacy statement.
  • Our lawful basis for using a host is our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

Other sites

Links you follow

Some pages link to research papers on other sites. If you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies. We tell your browser not to pass on which of our pages you came from.
