Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index (WOMAC)

The most widely used osteoarthritis-specific outcome measure worldwide. Validated for hip and knee OA. Gold standard for OA research.

  • 24 questions
  • 8-12 min
  • Score 0 to 96, lower is better
  • Gold Standard

About the WOMAC

Who it is for

Patients with hip or knee osteoarthritis, including those undergoing arthroplasty. Standard for OA clinical trials.

What it measures

Three subscales assess pain during activities, joint stiffness, and difficulty with physical functions. Specific to lower limb OA.

Sections

  • Pain (5 items)
  • Stiffness (2 items)
  • Physical Function (17 items)

Reading your score

Higher scores indicate worse symptoms. Can be normalized to 0-100. Subscales reported separately or as aggregate.

Strengths

  • Gold standard for OA research
  • Extensively validated worldwide
  • Accepted by regulatory agencies
  • Subscale and total scoring options

Limitations

  • License required for use
  • Less responsive in young active patients
  • Not validated for acute injuries
  • Hip/knee combined - not knee-specific

Source

Bellamy N, et al. Validation study of WOMAC: a health status instrument for measuring clinically important patient relevant outcomes. J Rheumatol. 1988;15(12):1833-1840. PubMed
