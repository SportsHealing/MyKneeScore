Paediatrics · General Knee

Pediatric Simple Knee Value (Pedi-SKV)

One question: how your knee compares with a normal knee, from 0 to 100. Developed and tested in children and teenagers.

  • 1 question
  • Under 1 min
  • Score 0 to 100, higher is better
  • Validated
All paediatric scores

About the Pedi-SKV

Who it is for

Children and teenagers with a knee problem, for a very quick check or to track progress between longer questionnaires.

What it measures

Your overall view of your knee, as a percentage of a normal knee.

Sections

  • Single overall rating

Reading your score

100 is a normal knee. Your answer is your score. Lower numbers mean your knee feels further from normal.

Strengths

  • Takes seconds to complete
  • Easy for children to understand
  • In its validation study it agreed strongly with the Pedi-IKDC and Lysholm scores, and picked up change after surgery

Limitations

  • A single rating cannot show which part of knee function is affected
  • Validated in a small study (44 patients)

Source

Marot V, Vilette H, Dalmas Y, Justo A, Reina N, Cavaignac E, Berard E, Accadbled F. Pediatric Simple Knee Value: a simple patient-reported outcome measure for the knee. J Child Orthop. 2021. Read the paper
